# Imagery Art Direction (style gate)

Run this BEFORE any imagery asset production. Output: `stages/imagery-style/art-direction.md` plus a moodboard and 2–3 style frames. The gate is approved only when the user approves the style frames, not just the written brief.

## Brief template

1. **Medium** — pick one primary, one optional accent:
   - Realistic photography
   - Illustration (state rendering: flat, line-art, textured/painterly, mosaic/collage)
   - Hybrid (e.g. photography with line-art overlay, or illustration-to-photo transitions)
   State why the medium fits the brand territories and the audience.
2. **Treatment** — the uniform finish applied to every asset:
   - Grading target (warmth, contrast, saturation; reference 1–2 graded examples)
   - Overlay policy for text-bearing placements (e.g. warm dark overlay, strength %, gradient direction)
   - Grain/texture, duotone vs full color, border/duplication rules
3. **Subject mix** — explicit ratios, e.g.:
   - Place/nature vs human faces vs detail shots (e.g. 50/30/20)
   - Candid vs directed ratio (e.g. 70/30)
   - Representation rules (age, ethnicity, ability) matched to the audience — for a 50+ audience, show 50+ faces, not stock twenty-somethings
4. **Mood words** — 3–5 words every asset must pass ("does this feel X?").
5. **Crop ratios + safe zones** per channel: hero (e.g. 21:9 or 16:9), card (4:3 or 1:1), social (1:1, 4:5, 9:16), avatar. Mark text-safe zones on the template.
6. **Licensing policy** — stock (which libraries, license type), AI-generated (disclosure/consistency risk), or commissioned; provenance recorded per asset.

## Style frames

Produce 2–3 fully-realized example images (hero + card + one secondary use) applying the brief exactly. These lock the style; batch production starts only after approval. Archive rejected frames in `stages/imagery-style/old/`.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Medium chosen with rationale tied to approved strategy
- [ ] Treatment specified concretely (values, not adjectives)
- [ ] Subject mix has numeric ratios and representation rules
- [ ] Mood words pass/fail test is usable by a reviewer
- [ ] Crop ratios + safe zones documented per channel
- [ ] Licensing policy stated
- [ ] 2–3 style frames approved by the user
