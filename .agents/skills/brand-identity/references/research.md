# Research mode (route `brand-research`)

Research brand, corporate identity, corporate design, competitor and design-system references before recommending. Separate evidence from interpretation; end with design implications and the next decision the user should make.

## Priorities

1. Inspect user-provided assets and local references first. For supplied website references, inspect the actual desktop/mobile presentation where possible and record specific principles to adapt or avoid; inaccessible pages stay unverified. Reuse the website's `design/reference-review.md` instead of repeating completed research; a narrow site-reference pass follows `.agents/skills/brand-website-designer-builder/references/reference-sites.md`.
2. Search official brand guidelines, design systems and press/media kits.
3. Compare 3-5 category leaders or competitors in the target country/region when available.
4. Mine `awesome-design/README.md` and `awesome-design/design-md/*/DESIGN.md`, when present, for section structure, token naming, component-state conventions, do/don't guardrails, responsive behaviour and brand signatures.
5. Include accessibility references (contrast, component states) and font licensing sources (Google Fonts, Fontsource, Adobe Fonts, foundries) when fonts are recommended.

## Benchmark notes per source

What the brand signals; country/region and segment relevance; primary and interaction colours; type personality; layout density; shape/radius language; motion/elevation style; signature component or asset; what not to copy. Record each source with its date.

## Output

Source list; repeated design patterns; category conventions; differentiation opportunities; accessibility and consistency risks.
