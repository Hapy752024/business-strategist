# Campaign review handoff

Apply when a selected campaign supplies a reviewed website brief or report. Resolve the registered Marketing root and read the campaign configuration, context, claims registry, website brief and latest reviewed report. Recheck their hashes/applicability; a private data-only preview is not an approved strategy decision. See repo-root `references/marketing-campaign-operations.md` for the local artifact contract.

Before page work, connect the audience and buying trigger to intent, offer, evidence, destination, CTA and confirmed customer outcome. State the visitor decision the page helps make. Use original useful material and preserve calculators, comparisons and other interactive value. Avoid producing keyword variants without distinct intent.

Prepare the complete mobile/desktop form-to-destination journey. Define required events, outcome IDs, consent behavior, test exclusions and provider deduplication. Advertising connection, measurement activation and verified lead receipt are distinct deliverables. A platform conversion count is not proof of qualified demand or causal uplift.

Use the current campaign claims registry and approved assets. Declared marker checks are supplementary: review unmarked claims, qualifications, guarantees and missing sources before release. Industry/jurisdiction overlays apply only when selected for this project. Missing owner-only facts become precise tasks with prepared material and completion evidence.

Record code/preview, deployment, confirmed destination receipt and mature commercial results separately. Reuse existing content/conversion, SEO, accessibility, performance, consent and launch checks. Feed observed friction and results back into the Marketing review; changes to strategy or external activation retain their existing decision/authorization process.
