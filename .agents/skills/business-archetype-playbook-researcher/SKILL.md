# Business Archetype Playbook Researcher

## Success Criteria
- **Quantitative:** triggers on >=90% of playbook/operator-research queries; completes in <=15 tool calls; collects from >=3 distinct source types; zero operator anecdotes presented as customer-demand evidence.
- **Qualitative:** source URLs, dates, and context are preserved; failure cases and selection bias are disclosed; operator patterns are separated from customer evidence.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` completely.
2. Reuse the active topic workspace and identify archetype, stage, geography, and constraints.
3. Search operator sources separately from customer-demand evidence.
4. Preserve source URLs, dates, context, failure cases, and selection bias.
5. Update the topic playbook artifact and manifest gaps.

## Output

Produce sourced patterns for segment choice, validation, first customers, low-cost marketing, pricing, launch, retention, scaling, anti-patterns, and applicability limits.
