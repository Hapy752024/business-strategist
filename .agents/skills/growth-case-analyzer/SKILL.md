# Growth Case Analyzer

## Success Criteria
- **Qualitative:** chronology, causal evidence, hypotheses, and hindsight judgment are separated; what should NOT be copied is stated; transferability limits are explicit.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` completely.
2. Verify current or disputed case facts from primary sources.
3. Separate chronology, causal evidence, hypotheses, and hindsight judgment.
4. Compare context before transferring a tactic to the user's company.
5. State what should not be copied.

## Output

Produce case mechanics, success/failure factors, evidence strength, transferability limits, applicable actions, and anti-patterns.
