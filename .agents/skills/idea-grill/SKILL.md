# Idea Grill

**Core validation sequence for a founder-chosen idea.**
1. `idea-grill` clarifies the idea, segment, core hypothesis, alternatives, workaround, urgency and riskiest assumption.
2. `evidence-scout`, invoked next with the Skill tool, collects source-grounded evidence (topic-led and entity-led customer voice).
3. `challenge-review` (customer-perspective mode) constructs evidence-grounded buying contexts.
4. `competitor-research` (landscape mode) separates same-market competitors, similar companies and capability references.
5. `opportunity-risk-designer` ranks risks and designs low-cost tests. Only the competitive-market lane may support whitespace claims.

When industry, business model and target customer are clear, run `precedent-research` (playbook mode) before strategic or launch recommendations.
Substantive validation includes an international commercial-precedent pass (repo-root `references/opportunity-research-approaches.md`) or states why it does not apply.
Intake rules are in [workflow](references/workflow.md): repo-root `references/strategic-positioning.md`, repo-root `references/interview-recruitment.md`, repo-root `references/case-insights.md`.
Founder constraints and preferences are inputs, not market evidence.
Recruitment planning is allowed before the pain gate; launches remain gated.
For a topic without a chosen idea, route to market-problem-discovery.

**Pain-first back-track.** When the founder arrives with a solution, first pin the customer segment (`market_research/customer_segments/`), the journey (`market_research/customer_journey/`) and the pains along it (`market_research/pain_points/`), validate those pains with web evidence, and only then pressure-test the solution. Solution-first grilling without that foundation is out of scope.

## Success Criteria
- **Qualitative:** the output hypothesis is specific enough to generate search terms; the segment is narrow enough to find and interview this week; solution language is stripped from search targets.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` completely.
2. For solution-first requests, back-track to segment, journey and pains first.
3. Ask one focused question at a time only for the remaining minimum inputs.
4. Write or update intake artifacts under `market_research/` (segments, journey, pains) and `strategy/intake/`.
5. Separate evidence, assumptions, counter-hypotheses and unknowns.
6. If the founder targets venture scale or asks about investors, assess investor-topic activity separately under repo-root `references/vc-investment-signals.md`.
7. Stop before customer-evidence collection when the segment or painful job is not researchable.

## Output

Open every output with a `## Strongest reason this fails` section tied to a cited claim, an `{{assumption.<id>}}` or a named unknown with its test; never open with what is promising, and hold correct findings under pushback (repo-root `references/research-coaching.md`, truthful stance). Produce a core hypothesis, assumptions, counter-hypotheses, customer-language search terms, riskiest assumption and next evidence command.
