# Product-Marketing Context

Fill this in once per product and read it before any positioning, messaging, website, social or campaign work. Pattern adapted from the shared-context idea in `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (MIT; https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills, retrieved 2026-10-31); the sections are this repository's own.

Rule: every claim below cites an evidence reference (case insights section, VOC table row, decision memo line, interview, or a dated source). A field without evidence stays `unknown` or `assumption` and is not used as a marketing claim. Do not fill gaps with plausible wording.

Evidence sources in this repo: `cases/<id>/case_insights.md`, the VOC table and decision memo (built by `scripts/evidence_scout/build_decision_memo.py`), the claim ledger, and `scripts/marketing/claims.py` for claim status.

Save as `product-marketing-context.md` in the project's marketing workspace and record the date and who confirmed it.
