# Precedent Research

One skill, one mode per former precedent specialist. The route ID picks the mode; read only that mode's reference.

| Route ID | Mode | Use when | Read |

| `growth-case-analyzer` | case | analysis of named companies that succeeded, failed, scaled or declined | [case](references/case.md) |

## Standing rules

- **Operator lessons are not demand evidence.** Founder stories, company cases and foreign success show mechanisms and transfer conditions, never that the user's customers have the pain or will pay. Keep them out of evidence records.
- Search before answering; prefer primary sources and preserve source URLs, dates and context. Label secondary interpretation and anonymous posts as such.
- Disclose survivorship and selection bias; include failed or contrasting cases and alternative explanations.
- Separate what happened, what evidence supports it, what likely caused it and what is popular narrative; avoid hindsight certainty.
- Compare segment, geography, price, regulation, timing and founder resources before transferring a tactic; state what transfers, what needs adaptation, what not to copy and the cheapest local test.
- Followers, funding, publicity and current scale do not establish success or explain how first customers were won.
- Apply repo-root `references/customer-voice.md` for query design before broad collection.
- Say "I don't know" when a claimed outcome cannot be verified; ask one focused question only when the answer changes the analysis.

## Shared references

- For substantive Business research apply repo-root `references/research-data-and-precedents.md`: official statistics and studies chosen by the decision, international applicability and investor signals; metadata is discovery until original findings are reviewed. When needed, add repo-root `references/institutional-and-university-sources.md`.
- For a registered Business case, pass applicable lessons, transfer limits and answered founder questions to the case-insights owner under repo-root `references/case-insights.md`; keep the sourced playbook or case analysis separate.

## Output

Return the output listed in the mode reference: playbook patterns with an applicability matrix and next tests, or per-company case lessons with what to copy and what to avoid. Always end with the dated source list.
