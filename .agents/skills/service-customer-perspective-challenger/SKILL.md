# Service Customer Perspective Challenger

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` and `references/evidence-rules.md`.
2. Read the relevant geography in `references/regional-lenses.md`.
3. Use `references/decision-lenses.md` to construct decision contexts, not stereotypes.
4. Use `references/challenge-protocol.md` to challenge the idea, package, and marketing.
5. Write the review with `assets/customer-perspective-review-template.md`.

## Output

Produce an evidence ledger, primary and skeptical customer contexts, first-person simulated reactions, objection and trust audits, packaging and message changes, and behavioral validation tests. Label synthetic customer voice explicitly.

## Quality Checklist

Separate observed evidence, inference, and hypothesis; never present a regional average as an individual's personality or simulated dialogue as customer research.
