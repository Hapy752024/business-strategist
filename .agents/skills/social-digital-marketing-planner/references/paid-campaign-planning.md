

# Paid campaign planning

Use for paid plans and budget questions. Retrieve current platform documentation before quoting thresholds; figures are planning anchors checked 2026-09-24 and must be re-verified and dated.

## Preconditions
Conversion path live and measured (events, consent, UTM), message learning from smoke test or organic work, and unit economics defining affordable CPA. Without retained-value evidence, make it a capped learning experiment with unlock gates.

## Channel selection
- Google Search: explicit problem/category intent; verify with keyword research and start with problem terms.
- Performance Max: only with a proven conversion signal and sufficient feed/assets; expect about six weeks before judging.
- Meta: consumer/local demand creation and retargeting; needs creative volume.
- LinkedIn: narrow B2B roles/high-value segments; high CPC.
- TikTok/YouTube: discovery and education with a sustained native video cadence.
Choose one primary and at most one supporting channel.

## Learning feasibility
- Meta: about 50 optimization events per ad set per week; approximate daily budget = target CPA × 50 ÷ 7. Fewer ad sets pool events.
- Google Smart Bidding: judge Target CPA after roughly 30 conversions and Target ROAS after roughly 50; learning can span one to two conversion cycles. AI Max for Search has cited budget floors near $50/day.
If the formula exceeds affordable spend, use an upstream event, consolidate, extend the window or reject the channel. Do not spend more to satisfy the algorithm.

## Structure and creative
One campaign per objective; one ad set/ad group per audience or intent; three to five ads each; one landing page per message variant. Test smoke-test pain frames across static, short video and carousel, changing one variable at a time.

## Measurement and stop rules
Use consent-aware analytics, UTMs and offline conversion import for booked/attended calls. Record total learning budget, daily cap, duration and decision in `budget-approval.json`; founder approval is required before launch. Stop when CPA exceeds the affordable ceiling after minimum sample, qualified-lead rate misses threshold, or the landing page falls below smoke-test baseline. Recheck Meta Business Help and Google Ads Help each run.
