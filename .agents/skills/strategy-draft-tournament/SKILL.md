# Strategy Draft Tournament

## Success Criteria

- **Quantitative:** 3-4 drafts with genuinely different mechanisms (not cosmetic variants); every judge score cites a registry lens or named assumption; at most 2 feedback rounds before converging or escalating to the user; winner or top-2 synthesis always produced.
- **Qualitative:** drafts differ in mechanism, not wording; the judge challenges rather than approves; cross-pollination visibly moves ideas between rounds; the final pick names what would falsify it.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` for the full protocol.
2. Read `references/judge-lenses.md` for the scoring rubric and evidence lenses.
3. Frame the decision question, constraints, and evidence pack before spawning any draft.
4. Spawn 3-4 parallel draft agents with distinct mandates; then judge, cross-pollinate, and converge.
5. Deliver the winner (or synthesized top-2) with scores, decisive tradeoffs, and the cheapest falsifying test.

## Quality Checklist

Drafts differ in mechanism, not tone; every judge score cites a lens or named assumption; no draft invents demand; sequencing and customer-trust lenses were applied; feedback rounds grafted ideas across drafts; cost stayed proportional to decision value.

## Output

Produce the decision frame, the draft set with one-line mechanisms, the judge scorecard, the feedback-round diff (if run), the selected or synthesized recommendation, its evidence gaps, and the cheapest test that would change the pick.
