## Round 2 — local-case designs converge

A borrowed B's identity-only registry and C's scoped-assessment taxonomy. B borrowed explicit visibility for EN/ZH, Arabic doctors, 55+, broker acquisition and broad personal cover. Both replaced digest-only correction handling with consumer lookup, stale assessment and scoped review; C challenged indirect dependencies and interrupted view/prose updates.

The judge verified bounded follow-up fixes and scored A **84**, B **87**. Both passed the eight insurance-grounded paper scenarios. A was then a central-assessment research option with full independent progression deferred. B was the stronger fit for ongoing independently resumed research.

This was a design-level stop for the scope then understood. It did not establish implemented behavior, migration safety or user-tested usability.
