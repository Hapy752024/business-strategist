## Blocking gaps shared by the finalists

1. **Correction propagation:** specify one source/claim correction, the consumer lookup, stale marking, affected conclusions/admission, review responsibility and reopening rule. Frozen evidence stays intact: distinguish a replacement/correction record from mutating historical bytes. A digest detects change only when checked; it cannot identify or reassess consumers by itself.
2. **Authority and Chinese-only resume:** name the authoritative home for identity, disposition, next research action, readiness, evidence binding and history. Walk through the exact files read/written when continuing Chinese. Motor state must remain unchanged. Root summaries may refresh; a shared file cannot overwrite an unrelated branch's state. State whether the overlay supports a narrow research continuation or full stage progression.
3. **Migration scope:** provide actual-file destinations and treatments, including old current-looking recommendations and cross-cutting documents. Separate new synthesis from moved original material. Include ambiguous mappings and missing coverage rather than silently assigning everything.

These require design answers now; they do not require implementing new software during this review.
