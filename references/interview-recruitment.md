# Recruit the first interviewees

Read when an interview, discovery test, first-customer or strategic GTM plan is requested. Apply to the actual idea and founder access. Interview recruitment planning is permitted before problem validation; it must not be routed as a product launch just because it includes a simple research invitation page.

Write/update `market_research/interviews/recruitment-plan.md`, linked from README and any GTM/risk plan. If the request is a narrow standalone answer, cover the applicable fields in that answer without creating a project.

1. Define whom to interview from a recent triggering incident, job, role, decision authority, geography and exclusions. Keep provider/adviser voices distinct from customer voices. No existing workaround can mean inability or avoidance; investigate consequences rather than automatically excluding the person.
2. Record founder network, languages, available time, budget and participant-compensation preference as confirmed/assumed/unknown. With zero contacts, research a cold start route. Never assume an accountant, association or supplier will introduce customers. Honor a no-participant-payment constraint: no cash, vouchers, gifts or equivalent rewards for attendance, and no compensated panel fallback. Distribution/recruiter fees are a separate cost category, not automatically authorized. Persist explicit preferences in coaching state and remove contradictory current recommendations.
3. Search current public sources for at least two viable routes when available. Give actual destination URLs, checked dates, audience-fit evidence, and the exact discovery mechanism: directory filters → relevant businesses → official role/contact endpoint; association → permission request to forward an invitation; relevant venue/community → organizer's participation rules; panel → eligibility screener and verified cost. A directory listing is a prospect hypothesis, not proof of pain or consent.
4. Specify the first bounded candidate batch, founder steps, invitation copy and reply mechanism, independent eligibility screening, scheduling, attendance and anonymized logging. Request opt-in forwarding rather than access to client lists. Preserve permitted use, community rules and applicable outreach/privacy requirements; public availability does not authorize bulk personal-data harvesting. No outreach is sent by the agent without explicit authorization.
5. Compare founder hours, cash/incentives, permission/access barriers, bias and fallback. Mark cost estimates and response assumptions as estimates. Panel recruiting fees and participant incentives are separate; check country/occupation coverage. Do not imply a platform guarantees qualified participants.
6. Define a learning funnel: identified → invited/exposed → responded → eligible → booked → attended → usable incident. Include denominators, cohort/channel, cost per eligible attended interview and rejection reasons. Set proposed batch-specific change/stop thresholds with a rationale; they are learning budgets, not statistical validation or universal response benchmarks.
7. Evaluate an honest research invitation page/form as a screening and booking tool. Specify how suitable visitors reach it. An isolated website creates no traffic. Distinguish **recruitment test** (can we reach eligible interviewees?), **demand test** (will a suitable customer take a meaningful step for this offer/fee?), and **sales/pilot** (actual authorized delivery and commercial behavior). Clicks, email addresses, incentivized attendance and waitlists do not establish willingness to pay.

Primary guidance checked 2026-09-13: [GOV.UK: find research participants](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/find-user-research-participants) supports explicit criteria and recruiting through relevant bodies, venues or agencies; [DfE: self recruitment](https://user-research.education.gov.uk/guidance/recruiting-participants/self-recruitment) informs organizational contact discovery and permission. These are methods, not evidence that a particular venture's target group is reachable. Verify idea-specific destinations each run.

## Choose a route that reaches the actual segment

Compare applicable options rather than prescribing a website for every idea: public business contacts; brief visits to accessible business premises; opt-in forwarding by an association, supplier or adviser; moderator-approved niche community posts; a useful ungated resource with an optional interview invitation; participant-forwarded invitations; or an honest pre-launch page supplied by a specific distribution channel. Paid recruitment advertising is distinct from paying interviewees, but requires its own budget and platform suitability. A founder with no audience cannot reproduce an audience-driven launch merely by copying its page.

Verify volunteers independently of compensation: business/site or other proportionate role evidence, relevant decision responsibility, geography, a recent incident described in their own words, and consistency when reconstructing the journey. Keep the pain answer open; do not disclose an answer key that rewards claimed distress. Include well-served negative cases, deduplicate respondents/businesses, and label referrals and channel clusters. Do not request sensitive financial credentials or identity documents as a default. Neither unpaid volunteering nor paid attendance establishes fit or willingness to buy.

Founder mechanisms checked 2026-09-13: [Buffer's founder account, 16 Feb 2011](https://buffer.com/resources/idea-to-paying-customers-in-7-weeks-how-we-did-it/) connects a landing page to Twitter distribution and email feedback; [Faire founder interview, 2019](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/max-rhodes-of-faire-with-anu-hariharan-on-scaling-and-fundraising/) describes personal retailer selling with a printed product sheet; [Zūm founder interview](https://review.firstround.com/zums-path-to-product-market-fit/) connects a mock site to a relevant local parent group. These are operator analogs, not proof of target-segment demand or universal channel effectiveness. Preserve network, geography, product and regulatory differences when adapting them.
