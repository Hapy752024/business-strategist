# Marketing module

Owns message, offer application, campaign, channel and marketing strategy outputs. Standalone work requires an established-business brief or position supplied by the user. Venture-linked work consumes a selected current Business decision and retains its evidence and stage gates. Before using an imported decision, compare the user's current task with its recorded `approved_for` and `applicability`; stop for owner review if scope differs or its limitations matter. A current file hash establishes source freshness, not fit for this use. Tie audience claims to evidence, label hypotheses, state the test and define how results change the next action.

Consume brand voice (`voice.json`) and the keyword map through imported decisions with purposes `brand_voice` and `keyword_map`. When the keyword map is absent, prepare it from the brief and available evidence using `marketing-strategy-builder`; ask the owner only for private first-hand facts, access, or an actual choice that cannot be inferred. Draft voice guidance can be proposed from supplied material and must be marked for owner review where identity or first-hand claims are uncertain.

For selected execution work, use the optional hash-bound `marketing/execution-plan.json` contract in `references/marketing-execution.md`. Prepare concrete agent-owned work and researched owner/third-party packets, including exact destinations and completion evidence. A required owner or publisher step remains pending until its own criterion is observed; do not describe sent outreach as a backlink or traffic gain. A focused marketing edit does not automatically create an execution plan.
