# Marketing module

Owns message, offer application, campaign, channel and marketing strategy outputs. Standalone work requires an established-business brief or position supplied by the user. Venture-linked work consumes a selected current Business decision and retains its evidence and stage gates. Before using an imported decision, compare the user's current task with its recorded `approved_for` and `applicability`; stop for owner review if scope differs or its limitations matter. A current file hash establishes source freshness, not fit for this use. Tie audience claims to evidence, label hypotheses, state the test and define how results change the next action.

Consume brand voice (`voice.json`) and the keyword map through imported decisions with purposes `brand_voice` and `keyword_map`; when absent, record the gap as an owner action instead of inventing tone or keywords.
