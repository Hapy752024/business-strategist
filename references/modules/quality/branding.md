# Branding output review rubric

Use this rubric after assets or guidelines are rendered. A strategy document alone cannot establish visual consistency.

## Good example

“The selected direction uses the approved wordmark, type scale and color tokens across the desktop landing page, mobile header and avatar. The small-size mark remains legible at 32 px; contrast was checked for body text and focus states. The German specimen exposes a missing umlaut glyph, so the font pairing is held for correction.”

Why it is good: it references approved inputs, checks real applications at relevant sizes, reports a concrete defect and keeps approval status explicit.

## Poor example

“The new logo looks premium and the brand feels consistent.”

Why it is poor: it offers preference without checking approved direction, actual uses, accessibility or unresolved issues.

## Review questions

- Do rendered assets follow the approved positioning and visual decisions?
- Are typography, multilingual glyphs, color contrast, spacing and responsive variants checked where relevant?
- Are source masters, exports, filenames and licenses traceable?
- Are disagreements and subjective alternatives documented rather than disguised as defects?
- Is delivery blocked on actual material defects and the required owner approval?
