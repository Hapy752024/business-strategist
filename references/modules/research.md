# Research module

Owns pressure-testing the user's stated idea and understanding a customer segment's pain points about a topic. Preserve the selected outcome through discovery, customer voice, market evidence, source review and bounded findings. Comparable companies, including foreign companies, are supporting evidence only when relevant to the user's question; importing or copying a business is not a default goal. Keep query intent, retrieval coverage, source provenance, observation versus interpretation, counterevidence and uncertainty attached through synthesis. Provider or access failure is a coverage gap, never evidence of absent pain/demand. Distinct approaches may run in parallel with isolated work packets, deduplicated sources and a single synthesis owner. Business commitments remain subject to the pain-first gate.
