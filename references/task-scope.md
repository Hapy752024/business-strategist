# Proportional task scope

Classify the requested work before loading specialist references. Task scope and response length are separate.

- **focused**: answer the specific question, including material evidence and uncertainty. Do not generate a strategy or workspace merely to answer.
- **execution**: produce only the requested copy, calendar, campaign or other deliverable. Reuse supplied context and approved strategy. Preserve audience, positioning, offer, budget and exclusions unless the user authorizes a change. Ask only for missing information that materially affects the deliverable.
- **strategy**: compare or design the broader strategy using the specialist workflow. Brevity does not waive research or safeguards.

The calling agent supplies `--intent` and `--task-scope` to the existing router when intent is understood. Phrase matching is a fallback and cannot interpret arbitrary negation, quoted examples or multi-step requests. Resolve material ambiguity before dispatch; do not ask the user to repeat an already clear choice. Existing workspace state is context, not authority to change a new request.

Prioritize the requested outcome over its components: a full strategy that includes copy, calendars or paid social remains strategy work. “Include” clauses elaborate the parent outcome; independently requested deliverables need scope/order resolution regardless of whether joined by a period, “and” or “then”. Explicit caller scope remains authoritative. Do not use execution keywords to downgrade a broader requested outcome.

For focused/execution work, read only the skill entry and directly linked references applicable to that task. A selected reference must still be read completely. Do not load a full strategic workflow just to extract a small execution procedure. No automatic founder study, competitive landscape, workspace creation or 90-day roadmap for routine execution. Verify new factual claims and current platform rules when needed; supplied wording does not authorize invented proof, guarantees or performance claims. Drafting never authorizes publishing, spending or contacting people.

Return the requested number and format of outputs. Explain a consequential constraint briefly; avoid unrelated sections and extra documents. Preserve evidence, privacy and owner-approval requirements at every scope.
