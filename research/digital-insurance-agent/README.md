# Digital Insurance Agent Research Package

This folder contains a go-to-market and marketing strategy for a multilingual hybrid insurance broker for Germany, focused on Mandarin, German, and English.

Core concept:

- Education-led acquisition through social media and community participation.
- Digital decision workflow with short learning units, personalized explanations, and offer upload/review.
- Human advisor review before final recommendation.
- Transparent incentive model and documented recommendation logic.
- Same advisor team remains responsible after sale for claims, contract changes, and renewal questions.
- App layer for post-sale value: contract overview, claim support, employer benefits inventory, and life-event prompts.

Files:

- `go-to-market-strategy.md`: positioning, segmentation, offer, funnel, launch plan, metrics, and risks.
- `customer-segments-and-journey.md`: target segments, customer journeys, decision triggers, pain points, objections, and interview targets.
- `social-media-playbook.md`: concrete Reddit, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Chinese-community execution.
- `digital-workflow-and-app.md`: product workflow, personalized video concept, human-in-loop model, and app feature backlog.
- `research-notes-and-sources.md`: evidence base, competitor/creator examples, compliance caveats, and source links.

Main recommendation:

Start with a narrow offer before building a broad broker app:

> "PKV or BU second opinion for high-earning non-native residents in Germany, with a Mandarin/German/English decision memo and advisor review before you sign."

The strongest evidence supports decision anxiety, broker distrust, and need for explainable recommendations. It does not yet prove that a full-service multilingual app will convert without first testing the second-opinion workflow.
