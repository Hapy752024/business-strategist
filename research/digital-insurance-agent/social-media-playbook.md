# Social Media And Digital Marketing Playbook

## 1. Platform Roles

| Platform | Role | Why It Fits | Main CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reddit | Trust building, pain mining, early leads | PKV/BU questions already appear in `r/Versicherung`, `r/Finanzen`, `r/germany`, `r/beamte` | "Use this checklist before signing" |

| SEO | Durable demand capture | High-intent PKV/BU queries exist | "PKV/BU offer review" |

## 2. Creator And Competitor Benchmarks

German examples to study:

- Versicherungen mit Kopf: strongest insurance-specific YouTube benchmark; uses Q&A, BU checks, contract checks, and consultation CTAs.
- Finanzfluss: broad finance education with insurance topics and strong trust from general investors.
- Finanztip: editorial authority and consumer-protection framing.
- Finance Made Simple, transparent-beraten, Der Finanzcop, Level V Finanz, PKV Klartext Dieter Homburg, buXperts: useful category references by specialization.

Expat/digital competitor references:

- Feather: English-first, expat-focused, clear quote/sign-up and call CTA.
- ottonova: insurer with English expat/private health positioning and app-native service.
- CLARK/Getsafe: digital broker/app positioning.
- CHECK24/Verivox: comparison and price-led funnels.

Chinese-language references found:

- Lazy Bear Insurance: Chinese-facing German insurance broker.
- Werks Makler: Chinese PKV page and guide content.
- STU Insurance / Care Concept / Expatrio / MAWISTA: student, expat, and entry health insurance content.
- Allianz Chinese agency pages and Chinese BU explainers exist, but the public Mandarin high-earner PKV/BU category is not crowded in the same way as English expat insurance.

Implication:

Surelius should not compete on generic "best insurance in Germany." It should compete on multilingual decision confidence, offer review, and after-sale accountability.

## 4. Reddit Execution

### Target Communities

- `r/Versicherung`
- `r/Finanzen`
- `r/germany`
- `r/askberliners`
- `r/berlin`
- `r/beamte`
- `r/OeffentlicherDienst`
- `r/expats`

### Operating Rules

- Do not spam links.
- Use a named expert account with clear disclosure: "I work on a multilingual insurance advisory concept; not legal/tax advice."
- Answer the question directly before mentioning any resource.
- Avoid "DM me" as the main behavior; moderators dislike lead fishing.
- Keep a public library of checklists and link only when the thread asks for it or rules allow.
- Track recurring questions in a content backlog.

### Weekly Reddit Routine

Monday:

- Search Reddit for `PKV`, `GKV`, `BU`, `Berufsunfähigkeit`, `anonyme Risikovoranfrage`, `Versicherungsmakler`, `expat health insurance Germany`.
- Save 10 threads and tag them by pain.

Tuesday-Thursday:

- Write 2-3 high-effort answers per day.
- Format answers as frameworks, not sales pitches.

Friday:

- Turn the best answer into:
  - one Instagram carousel,
  - one YouTube Short,
  - one blog FAQ,
  - one Mandarin post.

### Example Reddit Answer Structure

1. "The decision depends on 4 variables."
2. Explain variables: income stability, family plan, health history, retirement contribution tolerance.
3. Add "questions I would ask before signing."
4. Add "red flags in your offer."
5. Optional: "I made a public checklist here" if allowed.

### Reddit Lead Magnet

Create non-gated resources first:

- PKV offer review checklist.
- BU health-question prep checklist.
- "Questions to ask your broker."

Use gated lead capture only after trust is established.

## 5. Instagram Execution

### Account Structure

Create one primary multilingual brand account:

- Bio: "PKV, BU, and life insurance decisions in German, English, and Mandarin. Human-reviewed. No product-specific advisor bonuses."
- Link-in-bio:
  1. PKV second opinion.
  2. BU prep.
  3. Mandarin webinar.
  4. Free checklist.

Use language tagging in captions:

- `[DE]`
- `[EN]`
- `[中文]`

Do not create separate accounts until production capacity is proven. Separate accounts triple the workload.

### Cadence

Minimum viable cadence:

- 4 Reels/week.
- 2 carousels/week.
- 1 weekly Q&A Story.
- 1 monthly Live or webinar clip.

## 6. Mandarin Segment Execution

### Channel Hypothesis

The Mandarin high-earner audience will not be reached mainly through public Google SEO. Public Mandarin search results skew toward students, entry health insurance, and general guides. The better path is trusted community distribution:

- WeChat groups.
- WeChat official account articles.
- Xiaohongshu-style educational posts.
- Chinese professional associations.
- Chinese students transitioning to full-time jobs.
- Chinese engineers/researchers/PhDs/postdocs in Germany.
- Chinese tax advisors, relocation consultants, and immigration lawyers.

### Mandarin Content Angles

Use Mandarin explanations with German terms preserved:

- `德国私保(PKV)不是简单的价格比较`
- `公保(GKV)和私保(PKV): 高收入员工怎么判断?`
- `JAEG是什么? 超过收入线后你有哪些选择?`
- `职业伤残保险(BU): 为什么德国人这么重视?`
- `健康问卷(Gesundheitsfragen): 不要随便提交申请`
- `匿名风险预询(Anonyme Risikovoranfrage): 什么时候需要?`
- `保险经纪人(Makler)和保险代理(Vertreter)有什么区别?`
- `德国私保报销: Arzt-Rechnung, Einreichen, Erstattung`

### Mandarin Webinar

Monthly webinar:

Title:

> 德国高收入人群如何选择公保/私保和职业伤残保险?

Structure:

1. 10 minutes: German health insurance system.
2. 10 minutes: PKV/GKV decision scenarios.
3. 10 minutes: BU and health questions.
4. 10 minutes: broker incentives and red flags.
5. 20 minutes: anonymous Q&A.

CTA:

> Upload your offer for a Mandarin-reviewed decision memo.

### WeChat Distribution Tactics

- Partner with 10 group admins rather than cold-posting in 100 groups.
- Offer a free educational webinar for each group.
- Give admins a neutral checklist PDF, not a sales flyer.
- Track group/source manually with UTM links.
- Ask early customers for referrals into professional groups.
- Use Mandarin testimonials only with explicit consent and anonymization.

### Xiaohongshu-Style Post Formats

- "Germany insurance glossary" saved posts.
- "Mistakes I almost made when choosing PKV" narrative posts.
- "Before/after decision memo" screenshots with fake data.
- "German word of the week": JAEG, Selbstbehalt, Risikozuschlag, Ausschluss, Beihilfe.
- "High-earner scenario": single employee, married couple, child planning, self-employed.

## 8. Paid Plan

Do not spend meaningfully until:

- Landing page is live.
- Intake form works.
- Advisor calendar has capacity.
- Sample memo exists.
- Compliance wording is reviewed.

Initial paid tests:

- Retargeting only: site visitors and video engagers.
- Search ads for high-intent terms.
- Small-budget Meta/Instagram test against expats and high-income interest proxies.

Budget:

- Month 1: EUR 0-500, mostly retargeting.
- Month 2: EUR 1,000-2,000 if organic produces qualified calls.
- Month 3: EUR 3,000+ only if call quality and conversion are proven.

Stop if:

- Cost per qualified call is above EUR 250 for PKV/BU after landing-page fixes.
- Leads are mostly students or low-eligibility users.
- Call-to-application/revenue path is unclear.

## 10. Approval And Risk Checklist

Before publishing:

- No individualized advice in public comments.
- No unsupported savings/performance claims.
- No "guaranteed best product" wording.
- Compensation claims reviewed.
- Health and underwriting examples anonymized.
- Mandarin cultural context reviewed by native speaker.
- Captions included.
- Comments monitored for urgent personal cases.
