# TattooInsurance Validation Test Plan

## Next 7 Days

1. Studio-side willingness test

Hypothesis: reputable studios will sell a protection bundle if it reduces aftercare questions and does not imply poor quality.

Target: 10 studios across Madrid/Barcelona, Milan/Rome, Paris/Lyon, Berlin/Hamburg.

Action: pitch a one-page `TattooCare Protect` bundle with aftercare triage, complication reimbursement, and capped correction/removal contribution.

Success threshold: 3 studios agree to show it to customers or discuss revenue share.

Stop/pivot: fewer than 2 studios see it as credible, or they say it damages trust.

Budget/time: 2 days, under EUR 200.

2. Customer landing-page smoke test

Hypothesis: first-time and visible-tattoo customers click on protection for regret/removal and aftercare more than on infection/allergy insurance language.

Target: people searching country-localized removal/regret/aftercare terms.

Action: four localized landing pages, each with three message variants: removal/regret, aftercare/medical, and bad-result correction.

Success threshold: removal/regret or aftercare variant gets at least 2x CTR versus generic tattoo insurance.

Stop/pivot: generic interest below 1% CTR after enough impressions, or only free aftercare content converts.

Budget/time: 3 days, EUR 300-600 paid search/social.

3. Concierge claims simulation

Hypothesis: claims can be triaged with objective documents and a low dispute rate.

Target: 15 recent tattoo customers recruited from studios or communities.

Action: ask participants to classify hypothetical claims: infection, allergic reaction, bad linework, regret, fading, missed appointment, studio closure.

Success threshold: at least 80% agreement on what should be covered for medical and appointment events; less than 50% agreement on subjective regret confirms exclusion/waiting-period need.

Stop/pivot: customers expect all dissatisfaction to be covered and reject narrow terms.

Budget/time: 2 days, EUR 150-300 incentives.

4. Permanent makeup niche interview

Hypothesis: permanent makeup customers have higher protection willingness than general tattoo customers.

Target: 5 microblading/permanent makeup studios and 10 customers.

Action: interview on correction costs, infection anxiety, asymmetry concerns, and willingness to pay.

Success threshold: 5+ customers say they would pay at booking for correction/medical support, and 2 studios want to trial it.

Stop/pivot: same skepticism as tattoo studios or low perceived need.

Budget/time: 2 days, EUR 250.

## Decision Gate

Proceed only if a studio channel and one high-intent customer segment show willingness to pay. Otherwise, pivot from regulated insurance to a non-insurance aftercare membership or studio-backed guarantee workflow.
