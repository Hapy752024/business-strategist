# Founder GTM Playbooks — Research Synthesis

## Scope

- Archetypes: B2B SaaS, B2C SaaS, B2C fintech/insurtech, B2C professional services.
- Regions: Europe priority 1, United States priority 2, China priority 3.
- Decisions: how to test, launch, find first customers, use marketing and partnerships, reach repeatability, and scale.
- Retrieval date: 2026-07-05.

## Facts

- HEC Paris reports from interviews and a longitudinal study of 300+ B2B high-tech startups that personal sales has its greatest effect early, while broader marketing becomes more useful after validation. [S01]
- YC's MVP guidance recommends the least-effort test of the core hypothesis, treating early customers as education, and using service/manual delivery or meaningful commitments before full automation. [S13, S14]
- YC's Socialcam account shows that large download and traffic numbers can coexist with poor retention and no durable product-market fit. [S15]
- First Round's growth-lever interview describes mapping the customer journey, identifying its binding constraint, understanding customer mindset, and concentrating experiments there rather than operating a broad channel checklist. [S16]
- GetYourGuide's early supplier pull contributed to a pivot toward professional experiences; its later growth used search, supply sales, and travel-journey partnerships. [S06–S08]
- InterNations combined a specific expat community, online information, offline local events, curated membership, and local participation to build trust. [S09]
- EIOPA documents country-level innovation hubs, sandboxes, and a cross-border testing framework across the EEA. [S12]
- Xiaohongshu began with an expert-informed shopping guide, then developed UGC/reviews and community, and later direct commerce with licensed brands. [S18]

## Hypotheses derived from the evidence

- A startup should usually learn through one direct/manual motion before asking paid marketing or partners to scale an unclear offer.
- Partnerships are strongest when they control the customer's trigger, workflow, or trusted relationship and both parties can measure the handoff.
- B2C paid acquisition should follow activation and retention/repeat evidence because cheap attention can amplify poor customer quality.
- European expansion should be tested country by country; regulation, trust, language, support, and distribution are part of GTM.
- China should be treated as a new operating system for discovery, trust, commerce, and retention rather than a translated Western campaign.

## Judgments

- The most transferable founder mechanism is sequencing: manual learning → proof → one repeatable acquisition motion → broader marketing/partnership scale.
- “Launch” should mean a controlled cohort with observable value and support, not a one-day publicity event.
- Partnership announcements, waitlists, downloads, views, and followers should remain weak evidence until connected to retained customers and contribution economics.
- Fintech/insurtech GTM cannot be separated from licensing, promotions, advice/suitability, data, complaints, fraud/claims, and partner-control responsibilities.
- China recommendations in this research have lower confidence because direct founder and Chinese-language coverage was thinner.
